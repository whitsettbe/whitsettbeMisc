Chicago Cylinders

This tool generates a cylindrical representation of change ringing methods, based on the physical models adorning the University of Chicago Mitchell Tower ringing chamber.

Handstrokes and backstrokes are given separate (interleaved) columns. When places are made, the bells "flip" across the cylinder, due to the interleaving of handstrokes and backstrokes. These can be seen from above.

Number of bells:

Editable place notation:

Note: Comma-based symmetry notation is not yet supported. Letters are converted as A=1, B=2, C=3, etc. up to W=23.

Controls

Click and drag to rotate the view, scroll to zoom in and out, and use the slider to move up and down the cylinder.
