Chicago Cylinders

This tool generates cylindrical representations of change ringing methods, based on the physical models adorning the University of Chicago Mitchell Tower ringing chamber.

Description

Each combination of position and handstroke/backstroke is given a column. They are interleaved as 

1b, 2h, 3b, ..., Nh/b, Nb/h, ..., 3h, 2b, 1h
so that a plain hunt is a counterclockwise downward spiral. Here, the 1b column is emphasized.

When places are made, the bells "flip" across the cylinder, due to the interleaving of handstrokes and backstrokes. These can be seen from above.

Controls

Click and drag to rotate the view, scroll to zoom in and out, and use the slider to move up and down the cylinder.
